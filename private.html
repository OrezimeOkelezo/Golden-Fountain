CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: November 2026

This page explains the general approach Golden Fountain Table Water intends to follow when handling personal information shared through its website or customer enquiries. The final policy should be reviewed against the actual tools and processes used by the company.

Information you may provide

If you contact the company, you may choose to share details such as your name, telephone number, delivery location and the content of your enquiry. Please do not send payment credentials or other sensitive information through an ordinary website enquiry.

How information may be used

Information supplied in an enquiry may be used to respond to you, discuss product availability, coordinate a requested service and maintain relevant business records.

Sharing and protection

Personal information should only be accessible to people who need it to handle the relevant enquiry or service. Where service providers are used, information should be shared only as necessary and handled with appropriate safeguards. No website can promise absolute security.

Cookies and external services

This website may load resources such as web fonts from third-party providers. Those providers may receive technical information associated with the browser request. If analytics, advertising or other tracking tools are added, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

Your choices

You may choose what information to include when contacting the company. For a request about information you have shared, use the official Golden Fountain contact channel provided to you by the company.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website or the company's information-handling practices change. The date at the top of this page should be revised whenever a material update is made.
