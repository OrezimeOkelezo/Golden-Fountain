About Golden Fountain

OUR COMPANY

Refreshment with purpose.

Golden Fountain Table Water is a drinking-water brand based in Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria. Our product range is designed to serve different everyday needs, from individual refreshment to home, workplace and group use.

We believe customers deserve clear product information, dependable service and care at every point where our products are handled. That belief guides how we present our products and how we aim to serve our community.

What we focus on

Product choice. A range of convenient pack sizes for different settings and occasions.

Careful handling. Attention to packaging, storage and delivery as part of the customer experience.

Customer service. Clear communication and a responsive approach to product and supply enquiries.

Our commitment

We continue to work toward a consistent customer experience and responsible business practices. Product quality and regulatory statements should always be supported by current company documentation and applicable approvals.
